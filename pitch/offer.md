## Timelines

Shipping is in the critical path (design + UI build overlap), not a week-8 handoff.

### Ship — 6 weeks


| Weeks | Focus                                               |
| ----- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| 1–2   | Discovery, product bets, flows, wireframes          |
| 3–5   | Design system, hi-fi, **production UI in parallel** |
| 6     | Polish, validate, deliver frontend package          |


Gates: end of week 2 · mid week 5 · week 6 sign-off. Feedback SLA: 5 business days.

### Partner — 8 weeks + ongoing

Same spine as Ship, then **monthly product + design + UI shipping** (slices, reviews, iteration). Min 3 months after the build phase.
