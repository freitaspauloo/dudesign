## Bio

I founded DUDESIGN to solve a problem I kept seeing: tech companies building incredible products that looked nothing like the technology behind them.

We specialize in product design, AI-powered workflows, and brand experience for automotive, consumer tech, and retail. The work is strategic — we don't just make things look good, we make them communicate value.

Before DUDESIGN, I led design at **XIX3D**, working across product design, HMI, and brand systems for global clients.

Clients include **Audi, Samsung, 3M, Ford, Sony + Honda (Afeela), and Costco**.

Fortune 500 craft. Startup speed. Same standard now serving Series A–B teams that need to move without lowering the bar — product judgment, UX/UI, and a production interface in one engagement.
